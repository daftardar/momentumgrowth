What does an organisation need to be ready for funding?

Funding does not go to whoever merely asks. It goes to whoever can show they are ready: clear numbers, a plan for the money and the ability to repay.

Many organisations begin the funding journey by asking how much they need. The earlier question is whether their numbers are ready to be read. A funder looks for regular financial statements, understandable cash flows and a clear separation between the organisation’s money and its owners’.

Second, every amount requested should have a defined purpose and an expected result: what will be spent, when, and what will change afterwards in revenue or costs. A plan that links the amount to its impact is stronger than any presentation.

The condition that is never skipped is compliance, from zakat, tax and social insurance to corporate records. We recommend reviewing these with a qualified professional before applying to any funder.
