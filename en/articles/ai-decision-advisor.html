An AI advisor at the decision table: how can leadership benefit?

AI does not replace the decision, but it puts faster analysis and sharper questions in front of leadership before it decides.

AI can work as an advisor inside the company that attends meetings and sits at the decision table: it summarises the discussion as it unfolds, links the numbers and earlier reports to what is on the table, raises the questions that were missed, and sets out the alternatives with the risks of each.

Before the meeting it prepares a summary of the data and the latest developments, during it records the decisions, owners and deadlines, and afterwards it follows up on execution and reminds the team what was agreed. The meeting turns from open discussion into decisions that can be measured.

The fixed rule is that the decision stays with leadership, and the AI advisor offers an opinion that can be reviewed. Do not enter sensitive or confidential data into any tool before you understand where it is stored, and comply with the Kingdom’s personal data protection law.
