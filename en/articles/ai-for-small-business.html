How a small business can use AI without a tech team

AI is no longer reserved for large companies. The difference today is choosing well, not spending more.

Most small businesses start with the wrong question: which AI tool should we buy? The right question is: which task eats the team’s time and repeats every day? Answering customer enquiries, preparing proposals, following up candidates, writing reports. Those are the places where AI delivers within weeks, not years.

Second, start with one tool for one task and measure after a month: hours saved, customers or candidates gained. One tool that works on one task beats five that nobody uses.

And the condition not to skip: your customers’ data is your responsibility. Choose tools that store data securely and comply with the Kingdom’s Personal Data Protection Law, and never enter sensitive data into a tool before you know where it is stored.
